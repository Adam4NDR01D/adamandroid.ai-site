Merchant / Supplier
Blank apparel, promo items, undecorated goods — sourced by Sidebar on behalf of clients
  • Receives purchase order (PO) from Sidebar account manager
  • Produces or sources raw / blank goods — undecorated apparel, promo items, headwear, etc.
  • Ships raw goods to Sidebar warehouse on agreed lead time
  • Invoices Sidebar per PO terms — net 30 or net 60
  • Provides product specs: SKU, colorways, size run, unit cost at each tier

Human · Sidebar Account Manager
Sourcing + PO Management
Angela / Nicole — managing vendor relationships, pricing, and product onboarding
  • Receives client brief: product type, quantity, decoration specs, timeline, budget
  • Sources appropriate vendor(s) — checks existing vendor catalog first
  • Negotiates unit pricing with merchant based on quantity tier
  • Issues PO to merchant — tracked by email thread or spreadsheet (no formal PO system)
  • Confirms shipment ETA from vendor — manages lead time expectations

Pain Point · Pricing at Reorder
Pricing negotiated at initial contract time. When the same item is reordered months later at a new vendor cost, the Four51 sell price is often not updated — client sees old (lower) price while Sidebar absorbs the margin difference.
💡
Pricing Staleness Scanner (Bot-05)
  • Weekly automated scan: pulls all active Four51 items via SOAP API
  • Compares last price update date against most recent reorder / PO date
  • Flags items where reorder date is newer than last price change
  • Monday morning digest emailed to Angela + Nicole — review queue with current vs. expected price
  • Prevents the $4→$7 confrontation before it reaches the client

Inbound Receiving
Accepts raw goods from merchant — inspects, counts, and stores inventory
  • Receives merchant shipment at loading dock — confirms carrier and package count
  • Breaks down shipment — inspects for damage or shortage vs. PO
  • Counts and verifies units: SKU, size, color, decoration status (blank vs. printed)

Human · Sidebar Employee
Product Setup & Data Entry
Creates product records in Quantum — the catalog that powers Four51 and order validation
  • Creates new product record in Quantum ERP: item name, SKU, sizes, colors, unit cost
  • Sets sell price per contracted client tier in Four51 catalog
  • Assigns the item to the correct client account / buyer group
  • Uploads product photo(s) to Four51 for the storefront (if merchant provides them)
  • Assigns interop ID to the item — the identifier Zoho requires to route orders
  • Marks item as active in Four51 — it is now visible and purchasable by buyers
⚠
Root Cause · Interop ID Gaps
Interop ID assignment is manual and easily skipped at product creation. There is no required field or validation check. An item missing its interop ID works fine in Quantum and Four51 today — but silently fails in Zoho on August 1. ~30K items need to be audited.
✕
Known Gap · Missing Photos + Stale Data
Product photos are frequently missing — merchants don't always provide them; uploading is an extra manual step. Stale descriptions, wrong size runs, and outdated pricing go undetected because there is no recurring audit of catalog data quality.
💡
Interop ID Audit + Bulk Fill (Bot-02)
  • Jesse confirmed Four51 SOAP 1.1 API pulls all ~30K products in a single call
  • Script (audit.py, 30% built) flags every item with a missing or blank interop ID
  • Cross-references Quantum catalog export — auto-fills high-confidence SKU matches
  • Exports remaining gaps to Angela + Nicole for human approval
  • Batch writes filled IDs back to Four51 via Write API — validated before August 1
  • Can also surface photo-missing items as a secondary output

ERP · Product Catalog · Retiring August 1
Quantum — Catalog
Master product record: SKUs, pricing, interop IDs, inventory levels — the source of truth that feeds Four51
  • Holds master product catalog: all active items, variants, pricing tiers, and inventory positions
  • Acts as the authoritative source for what appears in each client's Four51 storefront
  • Syncs product data to Four51 (cadence and mechanism currently unclear — verify with Jesse)
  • Stores interop IDs — critical field for Zoho migration; currently often blank
  • Also manages pricing per buyer tier and per client account group
⚠
Migration Risk — Dual Role System
Quantum is simultaneously the catalog source AND the order processor. When it retires August 1, both roles transfer to Zoho. Catalog data quality issues (missing IDs, stale pricing) that are invisible in Quantum will cause immediate order failures in Zoho's stricter validation.

order submitted → Four51
Platform · B2B Storefront
Four51
Storefront, catalog, buyer account management — managed by Sidebar
  • Authenticates buyer against their account record
  • Serves catalog filtered to buyer's contracted items and pricing tier
  • Validates item is active and present in catalog with a complete SKU
  • Captures full order: buyer info, SKUs, quantities, PO number, timestamp
  • Assigns an order number — stores order in Four51 admin queue
  • Sends buyer an automated order confirmation email
⚠
Pain Point
Order sits in the Four51 admin queue with no automatic push downstream. A Sidebar employee must manually check and act — there is no notification, no SLA, no timer.
💡
Automated Order Detection
  • Build a polling integration (n8n or similar) that checks Four51 for new orders every 5–15 minutes
  • New order detected → auto-triggers downstream push to ERP immediately
  • No human needed to check the admin panel — orders flow through automatically
  • Anomaly alert: if no orders processed by end of business, ping Slack/Teams to confirm
  • Uses Four51 SOAP 1.1 API (Jesse confirmed available) or REST — no new credentials required

Human · Sidebar Employee
Order Entry
Routing orders from Four51 into Quantum ERP manually
  • Periodically opens Four51 admin panel to check for new orders (no push notification)
  • Reviews incoming order for completeness — SKUs, quantities, buyer account
  • Manually transcribes or copies order details from Four51
  • Opens Quantum ERP in a separate system (different login, different interface)
  • Manually keys the order into Quantum — line by line
  • Confirms order is received before closing Four51
⚠
Pain Point
Timing gap — if this person is busy, at lunch, or out, the order waits. No SLA, no escalation, no visibility into the delay.
✕
Known Failure
No audit trail of when a Four51 order was seen vs. when it was entered into Quantum. Delays are completely invisible to the buyer.
💡
Four51 → ERP Auto-Bridge

  • Full timestamp log at every step — order received, push attempted, push confirmed
  • Failed pushes trigger immediate Slack/Teams alert to on-call employee
  • Humans review exceptions only — not every order
  • Buyer-facing: optional auto-reply with order confirmation + expected timeline

Platform · Shipping & Fulfillment
ShipStation
Label generation, warehouse pick list, carrier tracking, EOD reporting
  • Receives fulfillment request from Quantum ERP
  • Selects carrier (FedEx, UPS, USPS) based on weight, destination, and service level
  • Generates shipping label — assigns tracking number
  • Creates pick list for warehouse staff
  • Monitors real-time carrier tracking — updates order status as package moves
  • End of each business day: generates EOD shipping report — all packages dispatched
pick list dispatched to warehouse
Human · Sidebar Warehouse
Warehouse Staff
Pick, pack, label, and hand off — physical fulfillment of the order
  • Receives pick list from ShipStation (printed sheet or mobile screen)
  • Locates items on warehouse shelving using item number and bin location
  • Picks correct item, variant, and quantity — verifies against pick list
  • Performs quality check — item matches order, no damage, correct decoration
  • Packs item into appropriate box with protective materials
  • Attaches ShipStation-printed label to outside of box
  • Brings sealed package to loading dock for courier pickup
package handed off at loading dock

Courier / Carrier

  • Scans package into carrier tracking system at dock pickup
  • Routes package through carrier network to destination
  • Pushes real-time tracking updates back to ShipStation
  • Marks as "delivered" upon confirmed handoff at destination
