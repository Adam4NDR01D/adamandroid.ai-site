3 · The loop

1
Sidebot-2 captures current pricing per variant and outputs the master price-variant list. Us
2
Send our captured list to G. It is the full current-state map: every product, size and buyer, with what Four51 charges now. It flags nothing as wrong, it is the baseline G checks against. Us
3
G compares our current prices against his source-of-truth prices and sends back the ones that need updating. Assumption G
4
Sidebot-3 combines G's list with the latest Sidebot-2 capture, but only if G's list is partial. Us
5
Sidebot-3 writes the new prices via the browser, one variant at a time, after approval. Us
6
Sidebot-3 produces the update report. Us

4 · How Sidebot-2 captures (Known, from the 2026-10-14 review)

  • Base price comes from the Price Breaks report, downloaded for each catalog.
  • The Variable Spec report shows which products have a SIZE spec.
  • SIZE markups are browser reads from each product's spec screen, only for products that have a SIZE spec. Markups are decimals stored to 6 places.
  • Markup type is fixed amount per unit for all 11 sized Uncle Bears products.
  • Each SIZE spec belongs to its own product, so editing one product's spec does not affect others.
  • The bot always reaches a product through its own link and never browses freely.
  • Guards: a completeness check for every product, a check that the size chart was scrolled to the end, and smaller-than-last runs are saved as drafts, not as the canonical capture.
  • SOAP is not part of the pricing path in either direction.

5 · How Sidebot-3 writes (fixed behaviour)

Inputs: the latest canonical Sidebot-2 capture, G's price update list, and this doc's rules.

  • Before each write, it re-reads the live price.
  • If the live price matches the capture, it writes the new price and records the before and after values.
Skip and flag: if the live price does not match the capture, Sidebot-3 skips that variant, flags it, and carries on. It never overwrites and never stops the run, so every other price still gets written. The likely cause is a hand edit, since markups are updated by hand today and account managers sometimes adjust size prices by hand. Before-values are kept for rollback.

6 · The update report

Tab · Updated

Product, size, buyer, before value, new value.

Tab · Warnings

Product, size, buyer and a link to the product in Four51; the captured price, live price and G's new price; and why it was skipped.

A human reviews each warning.
