4 · How Sidebot-2 captures (Known, from the 2026-10-14 review)

  • Base price comes from the Price Breaks report, downloaded for each catalog.
  • The Variable Spec report shows which products have a SIZE spec.
  • SIZE markups are browser reads from each product's spec screen, only for products that have a SIZE spec. Markups are decimals stored to 6 places.
  • Markup type is fixed amount per unit for all 11 sized Uncle Bears products.
  • Each SIZE spec belongs to its own product, so editing one product's spec does not affect others.
  • The bot always reaches a product through its own link and never browses freely.
  • Guards: a completeness check for every product, a check that the size chart was scrolled to the end, and smaller-than-last runs are saved as drafts, not as the canonical capture.
  • SOAP is not part of the pricing path in either direction.

5 · How Sidebot-3 writes (fixed behaviour)

Inputs: the latest canonical Sidebot-2 capture, G's price update list, and this doc's rules.

  • Before each write, it re-reads the live price.
  • If the live price matches the capture, it writes the new price and records the before and after values.
Skip and flag: if the live price does not match the capture, Sidebot-3 skips that variant, flags it, and carries on. It never overwrites and never stops the run, so every other price still gets written. The likely cause is a hand edit, since markups are updated by hand today and account managers sometimes adjust size prices by hand. Before-values are kept for rollback.

6 · The update report

Sidebot-3 generates this report after each price-correction run. It has two tabs: Updated, the prices that were changed, and Warnings, the variants that were skipped and need a person's attention.

Where the report is saved is still being decided. Sidebot-1 saves its deliverables into a top-level OUTPUT folder inside the bot's own package Known, so Sidebot-3 should match that, with an optional copy somewhere easier to share.

Tab · Updated

Product, size, buyer, before value, new value.

Tab · Warnings

Product, size, buyer and a link to the product in Four51; the captured price, live price and G's new price; and why it was skipped.

A human reviews each warning.

7 · Open questions

Q1(G) What format will G's price update list come in?
* Preferred: a CSV with one row per variant per buyer, keyed by the same variant ID our capture uses, carrying either base + markup or a total price. We are flexible on file type (CSV, spreadsheet or JSON all work); the matching variant ID is what matters. Can G match it?
Q2(G) Will his list be the full set or only the changed items? This decides whether step 4 is needed.
* Preferred: the full set. Sidebot-3 compares each of G's prices against our capture (what Four51 charges now): a match means no change, a difference is a change to write. Every captured variant either gets a price back or shows as missing, so we can confirm the list is complete. This checks coverage, not correctness; whether G's prices are right depends on his source data. Fallback: changed items only, with a total count so we can check we got everything he meant to send.
Q3(Brandi) Does each buyer get its own price, and where is that price set today?
* Preferred: Brandi clarifies first. The Sept 29 call showed per-buyer pricing exists (price varies by customer and by who owns the stock), with possible entry points in Books (purchase orders, customer price lists) and Four51 (price schedules assigned to buyer companies). B2B lot timing and retail point-of-sale add variations. Once we know where it is set, G can say whether his list carries it.
Q4(Nicole + Brandi) Partial inventory. Nicole asked (2026-10-21) for fractional quantities to be rounded up before they go to Four51. Is this part of the pricing fix, or a separate piece of work?
* Preferred: confirm scope first. This is inventory, not price, and Sidebot-2 and Sidebot-3 only handle prices. Get one real example of a fractional quantity to see where it comes from. If it is in scope, round up wherever the quantity is produced, before it is sent to Four51, likely in G's Creator layer (to confirm with G).
Q5(Brandi) How often do people hand-edit prices?
* Why it matters: it tells us how big the Warnings tab is likely to be, how often the pricing fix needs to run, and whether to build it on a schedule or on a trigger.
