1. Brand Philosophy
Human
Warm, grounded, personal. Real skin tones and hair colors are identity — not decoration. The person is the brand.
Space / Tech
Cool, expansive, precise. Blue takes over for infrastructure, AI, and systems thinking. Carl Sagan meets Silicon Valley.
The Sun
The logo is the sun. Orange is used sparingly — always as a focal point, never as wallpaper. The orange glasses are not a coincidence.
Monochrome
Design default. Everything sits on charcoal, grey, and white. Color is reserved for meaning, not decoration.

Always on

5. Callout Box Convention — color encodes meaning
Assumption
Needs confirmation before treating as fact. Flag for review.
Note / Info
Informational context or instruction. No action required.
Client Assumption
Our inference about the client. Review before sharing externally.
Risk / Blocker
Requires immediate attention. Do not proceed without resolution.
6. Logo & Mark
throat chakra
6 o'clock · bowtie
The logo is the sun. Position: throat chakra / 6 o'clock beneath the head, in a bowtie / halo relationship.
Color: Brand Orange #FF6600 only. Never recolor the mark.
The Pale Blue Dot (#6594BC) is its complementary counterpart — cool where the sun is warm, distance where the logo is presence.
The orange glasses are a live extension of the mark. In portrait-forward design, treat them as brand presence.
Clear space: minimum half the logo diameter on all sides.
7. Tone & Voice
Direct
No filler. No "as mentioned above." Every sentence earns its place.
Specific
Real numbers, real names, real stakes. Not vague generalities.
Transparent
Label assumptions explicitly. Distinguish confirmed from inferred.
Confident
Don't hedge unnecessarily. Own the recommendation.
Warm
Human, not casual. Precision with personality. The brand has a face.
