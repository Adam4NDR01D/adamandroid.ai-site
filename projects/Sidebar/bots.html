Bot-05
Pricing Staleness Scanner
Pain point: account managers forget to update sale prices in Four51 at reorder — customers discover wrong prices (the $4 → $7 scenario)
Priority 2 · Ongoing Risk
Stack Inside the Bot
Four51 SOAP API (products + pricing)
Quantum / reorder date export
Weekly cron (Monday AM)
Outlook API (digest email)
Per-size apparel tracking
Staleness threshold config
Every Monday: pulls all active Four51 items, compares last price update date against last reorder date. Items where reorder date > price update date get flagged. Digest sent to Angela + Nicole with item list, current price, and last change date.
Build Hours
28–42
hrs
$3,500–$5,500
Estimated project cost

Reorder date cross-reference: 6–10 hrs

Est. 2–3 hrs tuning in month 1, then near-zero. Weekly digest keeps account managers honest without requiring behavior change.

ROI within months of first incident prevented.
