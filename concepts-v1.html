Brand Explorations

Two new visual concepts: the traffic cone mark (crossing guard symbol, site/brand use) and the pale blue dot head (Substack/writing persona). Neither is finished — this is the sketch stage.

01 — The Cone Mark

Key fixes from v1: flat/rounded top (not a sharp wizard-hat point), squatter proportions, and a wider, heavier base plate that reads immediately as CONE not hat. The base is now visually dominant.

Variant A

Classic Two-Stripe

Flat top, wide base, two reflective stripes. Reads as traffic cone immediately.

Variant B · Recommended

Asterisk as Identity

The * IS the cone. Bold, centered, ownable. One stripe anchors the base.

Variant C

Outline / Minimal

Flat top cap + wide base outline removes the hat reading. Works on any background.

Cone Notes

  • v1 problem: sharp triangular point + tall narrow ratio = wizard hat. Fixed with: flat truncated top, squat 1.1:1 body ratio, and a heavy base plate that reads as cone base, not hat brim.
  • The cone + head mark relationship: head = who we are, cone = what we carry (signal, caution, guidance). Two marks, one brand.
  • The asterisk embedded in the cone is the same * from "Help Humans, Ride Robots*" — it's becoming a brand device throughout.
  • The head-wearing-a-cone variant (hat as joke/play) is a separate concept for later — noted.

02 — The Pale Blue Dot · Substack Persona

The head mark ends at y=215 in its 180×220 viewBox. The dot sits below the chin — where the throat is, where a bowtie knots — in clear space. Not overlapping. Not the mouth. Below.

Variant A

Bowtie — Close

Dot sits tight below the chin, like a bowtie knot just below the collar. Clear separation from head.

Variant B · Recommended

Throat Chakra · Animated

Head ends at y=215. Dot at y=252 — distinct space below the chin, like a bowtie. Asterisk orbits. Voice, presence, Earth.

Variant C

With Tagline

More air between chin and dot. Tagline sits quietly beneath. Substack header format.

Pale Blue Dot Notes

  • The outer ellipse of the head mark bottoms out at y=215 (cy=110 + ry=105). The dot sits at y=248–262 — well below, in its own space. Never overlapping.
  • The throat chakra in yogic tradition governs voice, truth, and authentic expression — exactly what the Substack is. The dot at the throat says: this is where we speak from.
  • Visually it reads like a bowtie knot — dressed, grounded, present. Not floating. Not flying away.
  • The orbiting white asterisk traces a path around the Earth. Slow, quiet attention to what's small and here.
  • This mark lives on Substack, not the homepage. It's the writing persona — a different register than the consulting brand.
  • "We speak for earthlings" — presence-forward, not opposition-forward. Here & now.

03 — Substack · The Writing Persona

Be Here.
Build Now.

The future gets all the attention. The present does all the work. This Substack is about showing up — for the humans who are here, the problems that are real, the tools that already exist and mostly go unused.

Not a critique of what's being built elsewhere. An invitation to notice what's needed right here. Lunch. Connection. A clear path through the noise.

"Let's build tools for humans." — the throughline.
