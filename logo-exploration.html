Design Notes

  • The left "Adam" side uses SVG fractal noise displacement to create organic, hand-drawn wobble — same geometry underneath, just roughened.
  • The right "Android" side is the same geometry rendered clean with the orange gradient — zero filter, perfect precision.
  • The gradient mask creates a blend zone in the middle — no hard cut — so the two natures feel like one unified mark.
  • The small center node (dot on the seam) represents the bridge point — the place where human and machine meet.
  • At small sizes (nav bar), the sketch vs. polish reads as texture contrast rather than an obvious split — it still holds together as a logo.
  • On "too strange": the asymmetry (different-sized eyes) is actually your strongest distinctive asset. At small sizes it reads as a face. At large sizes it rewards close attention. Keep it.

03 — On "Adam&droid" vs. "AdamAndroid"

Option A · Keep It Clean

AdamAndroid.ai

The domain does the work. .ai is already the signal. No ambiguity, full brand continuity with adamandroid.com.

Option B · Lean Into the Split

Adam&AI

Drops the "droid" entirely. "Adam & AI" — human + machine, built right into the name. Clean, contemporary, no trademark concerns. Works perfectly with the split-face logo concept.
